Naïma Luxury Cottage

A minimalist garden retreat

★ 4.97 · Superhost · 62 reviews

Kick back and relax in this calm, stylish cottage wrapped in a beautiful green tropical garden. The interior is a cosy, minimalist design with natural-stone floors and a terrace laid with historical cement tiles. Naïma sits in a tranquil, secluded spot surrounded by a unique green jungle — just steps from Sero Cristalberg, ideal for adventure hikes into the island's wild nature. Inside, a locally hand-crafted king-size bed in Caribbean teak, a skylight bathroom open to the stars, and a fully-equipped outdoor kitchen make it easy to settle in. A car rental is highly recommended for exploring the island at your own pace.

Inside Naïma

The cottage, room by room

An interior inspired by a cozy, minimalist look — with natural-stone floors throughout.

Bedroom

The bedroom includes a hand-made king-size bed crafted with Caribbean teak wood, comfortable closets, a ceiling fan, air-conditioning, and natural desert and tropical plants.

Outdoor kitchen

The outdoor, fully-equipped kitchen has a four-burner electric stove, small refrigerator, small freezer, cooking utensils, electric oven, coffee maker, toaster, water kettle and a natural-wood charcoal BBQ.

Bathroom

The spacious bathroom offers a two-person vanity, a rain shower and a skylight rooftop — plenty of natural light during the day and, at night, a gorgeous view of the dark blue sky and countless stars.
