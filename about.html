Meet Ana Méndez

Airbnb Superhost 4.97 ★ · 62 reviews 4 years hosting

Méndez Estates is personally hosted by Ana Méndez — an Airbnb Superhost with a genuine love of the outdoors and nature. She tends the garden herself, looks after George the cat and the property's chickens and tortoises, and personally arranges every detail of a guest's stay — from the welcome breakfast basket to a private chef dinner or a tip on the best hidden beach.

For Ana, hospitality in Aruba isn't a transaction. It's an invitation: come as a guest, leave feeling at home. Both villas sit together in the quiet heart of Aruba, minutes from the Casibari Rock Formation and within easy reach of the whole island — and whatever you need, Ana is a message away.
