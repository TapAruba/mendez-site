Maxwell Luxury Villa

Open-concept living

For families & groups · sleeps up to 8

Maxwell Luxury is a high-end, open-concept home designed for comfortable, memorable stays — with a spacious outdoor area surrounded by nature and a large private pool with a relaxing lounge. The open-air living room has a ceiling fan, comfortable couches and an eight-seat wooden table — relax after a meal with a glass of wine or a cold Balashi beer. Three air-conditioned suites offer excellent natural light: a master with a king bed, ceiling fan and Smart TV, plus two more bedrooms with queen beds (one also with a bunk bed). Ideal for families and groups seeking a premium experience in a peaceful, exclusive setting.

Inside Maxwell

The villa, space by space

Open-concept living that flows from a gourmet kitchen to a large private pool, wrapped in nature.

Living & pool

A spacious outdoor area surrounded by nature, with a large private pool and lounge. The open-air living room has a ceiling fan, comfortable couches and an eight-seat wooden table — perfect for a glass of wine or a cold Balashi beer after a meal.

Three suites

Three well-appointed, air-conditioned suites with excellent natural light. The master has a king-size bed, ceiling fan and Smart TV; the other two have queen beds, and one also includes a bunk bed.

Gourmet kitchen

A fully-equipped gourmet kitchen with a Smart TV, three-burner gas stove, large refrigerator, electric oven, coffee maker, toaster, kettle, blender, cooking utensils and a two-burner gas BBQ.

Two bathrooms

Two full bathrooms with hot showers, stocked with 100% cotton towels, bath soap, shampoo and hand soap.
